HOTEL POLICIES

Good to know
before you arrive.

A few simple policies to help you plan a smooth and comfortable stay.

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in from 2:00 PM, check-out until 12:00 PM. Early or late requests depend on availability.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before arrival. Later cancellations may be charged one night.

ID Required

A valid passport or national ID is required for every guest at check-in.

Children & Extra Beds

Children of all ages are welcome. Extra beds and cribs are available on request.

Pets

Pets are not permitted, with the exception of registered service animals.

Smoking

Sky Erbil Hotel is a non-smoking property; designated outdoor areas are available.
